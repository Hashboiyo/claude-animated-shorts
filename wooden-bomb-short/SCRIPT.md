## Facts with sources (user rule: jokes are researched real facts)

**The decoy was real practice, not legend:**
- Germans built decoy airfields in occupied Europe, including Holland. Documented example: the imitation of
  Eindhoven airfield at Oost-, West- en Middelbeers — hangars, runways, landing lights, barracks, and even a
  fake plane that drove up and down a rail when enemy aircraft approached (Atlas Obscura, "Imitation Airfield
  in Oost-, West- en Middelbeers").
- The British ran the same game: Colonel John Fisher Turner's decoy programme — K-sites (day dummy airfields
  with dummy aircraft), Q-sites (night light decoys), Starfish "Special Fire" sites; by war's end 237 decoys,
  and German bombers attacked them repeatedly throughout the war (Wikipedia "Starfish site"; BBC WW2 People's War).
- Operation Fortitude faked an entire army group with dummy aircraft/tanks for D-Day deception.

**The wooden bomb is a famous wartime story:**
- The classic telling (att. Wolfgang Falta via De Longe, retold 2021 by Forces News): a Dutch decoy "made almost
  entirely of wood, including hangars, gun positions, aircraft and vehicles … The day after it was finished, a
  solitary RAF plane flew over and circled the field once before dropping a large wooden bomb."
- Often told with "WOOD FOR WOOD" painted on the bomb. First appeared in print ~William L. Shirer, Berlin Diary.
- French historian Pierre-Antoine Courouble, *The Riddle of the Wooden Bombs* (2009, with Jean Dewaerheid),
  collected 300+ testimonies; star witness Lt. Col. Werner Thiel (Luftwaffe pilot) said in 2010 he saw wooden
  bombs marked "wood for wood" at a decoy/training airfield near Potsdam after a night raid, October 1943
  (War History Online; Soldier of Fortune; Commonplace Fun Facts).
- Mainstream view: unproven urban legend (no logbooks/O.K.W. records; Snopes rating "Unproven").

**Editorial line:** the video tells the famous Dutch story as the classic wartime tale (that's the user's prompt
and the joy of it). The *explanation* beat is fully factual perception science, and the *consequence* beat only
uses documented facts (real decoy war, Turner's hundreds of British fake airfields).
